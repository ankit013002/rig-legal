Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This policy explains what Rig ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. Rig is developed by PETALFORM LLC. Rig is built to be local first: the computers and components you add live on your device, and an account is optional.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address to sign you in and sync your data across your devices.
  • Your PC data. The computers and components you add. This is stored on your device. It is sent to our backend only if you are signed in, so it can sync across your devices.
  • Usage and analytics. Aggregated, product interaction events (for example, which screens are used) to understand and improve the app. Personal identifiers are removed at the analytics boundary. On the web, analytics runs only after you consent.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and error reports to find and fix problems.
  • Notifications. If you enable notifications, a device push token so we can deliver them.
  • Purchases. Your subscription and purchase status, to unlock paid features. Payment card details are handled by Apple and our payment processors, never by us.

How we use information

  • Operate the app and personalize hardware guidance to your machine.
  • Sync your data across your devices when you are signed in.
  • Process and restore subscriptions.
  • Diagnose problems and improve reliability and features.

Service providers

We share limited data with providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and storage.
  • PostHog for product analytics.
  • Sentry for crash and error reporting.
  • Stripe and RevenueCat for subscriptions and purchases.
  • Apple for the App Store and push notification delivery.

Tracking

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account from within the app, which removes your associated account data from our backend. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app. You can also request deletion by emailing us.

Children

Rig is rated 4+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@rigapp.dev.
