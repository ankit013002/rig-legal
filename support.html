Need help with Rig? We are happy to help.

Contact

Email support@rigapp.dev and we will get back to you. Including your device model and iOS version helps us answer faster.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Rig works offline and your PCs are saved on your device. An account is optional and only syncs your data across your devices.
  • How do I add my PC? Open the My PCs tab, tap Add your PC, then add your components. You can also load a sample PC to try everything.
  • Are the numbers real benchmarks? Performance and prices are labeled estimates for planning, not measured benchmarks or live quotes.
  • How do I manage or cancel a subscription? Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID in the App Store, under Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • How do I delete my account or data? Use Delete Account in the app, or email us and we will remove your data.
